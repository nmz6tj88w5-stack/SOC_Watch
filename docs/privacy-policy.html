Privacy Policy for SOC Watch

Last updated: September 26, 2026

SOC Watch ("the App") is a single-player idle/strategy game. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

SOC Watch does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not require an account or internet connection to play.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information. The App does not use cookies, and it does not transmit any data to us or to any third party over the network.

On-Device Data

The App stores your game progress (such as wave, budget, and upgrade levels) locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

Local Diagnostics Logging

The App uses Apple's os.Logger system to write basic gameplay diagnostic events (for example, when a wave is reached or a session starts) to your device's local system log. These logs stay entirely on your device, are never uploaded anywhere, and are only viewable locally through Apple's own developer tools (such as Console.app) during development. They contain no personal information.

Notifications

If you grant permission, the App may schedule local notifications on your device (for example, a reminder about your defenses). These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on-device and do not involve any remote push service or external server.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
