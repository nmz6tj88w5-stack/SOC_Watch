SOC Watch — Support

SOC Watch is a single-player idle/strategy game where you defend a Security Operations Center against incoming incident waves.

Need help or found a bug?

Contact us at yan@massart.eu.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?
No. SOC Watch has no sign-in or account system — your progress is saved automatically on your device.

Does the game show ads?
There's one optional rewarded video ad you can watch to double your offline progress. It's never required to play, and it never shows automatically.

I lost my progress after reinstalling the app — is that normal?
Yes. Progress is stored locally on your device only (not in an account), so it isn't preserved across reinstalls or device changes.
